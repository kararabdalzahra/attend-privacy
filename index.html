Privacy Policy

Attend is an offline attendance-scanning app for classroom use. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is stored, and what — if anything — ever leaves your device.

What Attend stores, and where

All class, student, and attendance data you enter into Attend is stored only on your device, in the app's private local storage. Attend does not operate a server and does not sync, upload, or back up this data anywhere.

Camera use

Attend uses your device's back camera to scan QR codes and, if you use the optional "Scan and record" feature, to record video of a scanning session. Camera frames used for scanning are processed on-device by Google's ML Kit barcode library and are never transmitted anywhere. Recorded video is saved directly to your device's storage and is never uploaded.

License activation (the only network request Attend makes)

Attend offers a free trial and an optional paid license. Activating a license key sends the license key and a basic device identifier (your phone's manufacturer and model) to Lemon Squeezy Inc., the payment and licensing provider, solely to validate the key. This happens once, at activation — Attend does not contact any server afterward. Lemon Squeezy's own handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

What Attend does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No cloud storage or backup of class/student data
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties

Data about students

Student names and ID numbers are entered by the teacher using Attend, not collected directly from students. This data stays on the teacher's device under the teacher's control, and the institution's own data-handling policies apply to how rosters are sourced and used.

Children's data

Attend is intended for use by teachers and institution staff, not by children directly. Any student data entered may relate to minors; it is handled the same as all other Attend data — stored locally only, never transmitted, and never used for any purpose beyond attendance tracking on the device where it was entered.

Data retention and deletion

Data persists on your device until you delete it within the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Attend. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored database. Files you've exported (CSV, PDF, video) to your Downloads or Movies folder are not removed automatically and can be deleted like any other file on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Data Subject Rights

Because Attend stores class and attendance data only on your own device, you are already in direct control of it — most rights below are exercised on-device, with no need to contact anyone.

Access & export

Open any class in Attend to view its roster and session history. Use the app's built-in export (CSV/PDF) to obtain a copy of any data at any time.

Correction

Edit or correct student names and IDs directly in Attend's "Edit students" screen. Changes apply immediately and locally.

Deletion

Delete individual students, sessions, or whole classes from within the app, or remove all app data at once via your device's Settings → Apps → Attend → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

License / activation data

The only data that ever leaves your device is the license key and device identifier sent to Lemon Squeezy at activation. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of that record, contact Lemon Squeezy directly: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

Questions or requests

For anything not covered above, or to ask a question about how Attend handles data, use the contact details below. Requests are normally handled within a reasonable time.
